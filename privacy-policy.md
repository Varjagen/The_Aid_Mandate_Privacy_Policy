# Privacy Policy

Version 1.1 · Effective date: 31 October 2026

Draft for testing builds. To be reviewed by a qualified lawyer before public release. Also shown in the game: More, then Privacy.

## In short

- The Aid Mandate has no account, no advertising, no in-game purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking.
- The game sends nothing on its own. It sends one thing only when you choose to: a bug report you write and send with Report a bug. You do not need an e-mail address or an account for that.
- Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. Deleting the game deletes them.
- If you send us feedback during testing, we receive only what you choose to send.

## Who is responsible

A Dove Takes Flight (Naut Engelsma), Oosterbeek, the Netherlands. Contact: ADoveTakesFlight@gmail.com.

## What is stored on your device

The game keeps these files in its own private storage on your device, so that it can save your place and remember your choices:

- your saved campaigns (the state of each game, the version of the game and the time it was saved);
- the record of journeys, finished campaigns and Directors removed (the Archive);
- your achievements, legacies and endings found, with the time each was earned;
- your settings (such as text size, sound, haptics and high contrast), small counters the game uses to adjust its hints, and the versions of these terms and this policy you have seen and accepted;
- sounds the game generates, kept so it starts faster.

None of this contains your name, contact details, location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or device identifiers. It never leaves your device through the game, and we cannot see it. A bug report you are still writing is kept only while the game is open.

On a computer, the game engine may also write technical files, such as logs, to the same folder; they also stay on your computer.

## What is sent from your device

Nothing, unless you send a bug report. The game has no analytics, advertising, crash reporting or tracking, and no other third-party software development kits (SDKs). It uses the internet only to send a report when you press Send.

## Bug reports

You can report a problem from the game: Report a bug, in the pause menu or under More. A report contains:

- what you write in it;
- unless you switch "Include game details" off: the game's version, the kind of device and its system version (for example "Android 14" and the phone model), the screen size, your text size, high-contrast and reduce-motion settings, where you opened the report, and, if a campaign is under way, the campaign, the week, the current card and the campaign code. With the details off, only the game's version and where you opened the report are sent.

The screen shows exactly these details before you send. A report contains no name, e-mail address, account, advertising or device identifier, or location, unless you write them yourself; please don't.

When you press Send, the report goes over an encrypted connection (HTTPS) to a small Google Apps Script program that runs under our Google account, which e-mails it to ADoveTakesFlight@gmail.com. Google provides that service and the Gmail account, and handles the connection under its own terms; like any website, it receives your device's IP address to answer the request. The e-mail we receive does not contain your IP address. Because a report carries no address, we cannot write back to you.

The game does not opt in to Android's automatic backup, so Android does not copy its data to a cloud backup.

## Permissions

On Android the game asks for two permissions: internet access, used only to send a bug report you choose to send, and vibration, used only for the optional haptic feedback (Settings, then Haptics). Neither is shown as a question: Android grants them when the game is installed. The game does not ask for access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files.

## Links to websites

"Want to help?" and Sources and real-world parallels contain links to other organisations' websites. A link opens in your browser only when you tap it. The game adds nothing about you to the link. The website you visit receives what any website receives from your browser, under that website's own privacy policy.

## The store you install from

If you install the game from an app store such as Google Play, the store processes information about your download and your device under its own privacy policy. Depending on your device's settings, Google may give us summary crash and performance reports for the app, which can include your device model and Android version. We use them only to fix problems. Test versions may be shared with you in other ways; whoever sends you the test version handles that under their own arrangements.

## Feedback during testing

Sending feedback is voluntary. You can send a bug report from the game (above), or write to ADoveTakesFlight@gmail.com. Both arrive as e-mails in a Gmail account, a service provided by Google. You decide what to send: for example a description of what happened, the campaign and week, a campaign code, screenshots or screen recordings (by e-mail). Please do not include personal information that is not needed, such as notifications or other apps visible in a screenshot.

We use bug reports and feedback only to understand problems and improve the game. We keep it until the test phase ends, and at most 12 months after we receive it, and then delete it. We do not sell it or share it, except with people who help us develop the game and are bound to keep it confidential. We process feedback on the basis of our legitimate interest in improving the game (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR).

If a moderated test session records your screen or voice, the person running the session will explain this and ask for your consent separately before recording (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR).

## Children

The game is intended for players aged 12 and older (the developer's intention, not an official age rating). The game itself collects no personal information from anyone; a bug report asks players not to write personal details. Feedback programmes are intended for adults; if a younger tester takes part, a parent or guardian should send any feedback by e-mail.

## How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Data on your device stays until you delete it. On Android: uninstall the game, or open your phone's Settings, then Apps, then The Aid Mandate, then Storage, then Clear storage. This deletes all saved campaigns, settings, achievements and legacies, and cannot be undone. We hold no copy.

Feedback and bug reports you sent us: ask us at ADoveTakesFlight@gmail.com to delete them. A bug report carries no name or address, so tell us roughly when you sent it and what it said, and we will find and delete it.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask us for access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict its use, and to complain to a data-protection authority: in the Netherlands the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens, or the authority in the country where you live. We hold personal information only if you put it in a bug report or send us feedback or contact us. To use these rights, write to ADoveTakesFlight@gmail.com.

## Security

Your progress is kept in the game's private storage, which other apps cannot read on a normal Android device. Saves carry a check value so the game can detect a damaged file; they are not encrypted. Bug reports travel over an encrypted connection. Anyone with access to your unlocked device, or to a computer where you play, may be able to open the game and see your progress.

## Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this policy, give it a new version number and show it in the game before you continue playing.
